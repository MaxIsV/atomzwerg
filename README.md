# Atomzwerg
Atomzwerg is a small project of two students at Hochschule Bremen made for studying purposes.

A top-down roguelike shooter set in a post-apocalyptic world following a nuclear power plant disaster. The player must avoid radioactive objects and the hazardous environment while destroying enemies to find their family.

---

## 🌌 Game Overview
* **Setting:** The world in the apocalypse after a nuclear catastrophe.
* **Graphic Style:** Pixel Art.
* **Animation Type:** Sprite frame-by-frame animation.

---

## ⚙️ Game Mechanics
* **☢️ Radiation Meter:** Tracks environmental hazards; players must avoid radioactive objects and areas.
* **💥 Enemy Combat:** Enemies can be actively attacked and eliminated.
* **🔫 Projectile System:** Weapons generate and shoot distinct projectiles.
* **🎁 Collectibles & Bonuses:** Weapons and power-up bonuses can be gathered throughout the game.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Components
* **🗂️ Scene-Based Levels:** Different levels are separated and managed as individual technical scenes.
* **🧳 Weapon Inventory:** A dedicated inventory system to store and manage collected weapons.
* **🤖 NPC Behavior:** Custom AI behavior and movement logic for enemies and non-player characters.
